## Page 3 — Approach (`/approach.html`)

**H1:** "Study how your operation runs, automate what pays, plan it in writing."

| Step | Heading | Core promise |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | Study how your operation actually runs | "The better the picture we have, the more we can solve — and the less guesswork ends up in the quote." Returns a written read on where the repetitive work is, ranked by cost, usually within two business days |
| 2 | Pick what's worth automating | A shortlist of what to automate and what to leave alone, each sized in hours saved or cost avoided |
| 3 | Deploy a supervised agent — or buy off the shelf | The plan comes in writing with the cost. "If the honest answer is 'buy this off-the-shelf product instead,' we write that down and tell you which one." |
| 4 | Deliver the plan and quote, then governance | "Every engagement ends the same way: a detailed plan and a quote." Supervised agents deliver the build. "The person who built it doesn't get to decide it works." |

Principles: supervised, not set loose / honest about existing solutions /
you own the output / plain language in writing / honest "no".

## Page 8 — Free Pre-Check (`/consultation.html`)

**H1:** "Let's find out whether a plan is viable for your business."

Purpose: lead collection plus the beginning of data collection. It gauges
whether a plan is even viable, and sells the paid $250 consultation. It is
**not** the consultation itself — the page says so explicitly.

Form fields, in order:

| # | Field | Type | Required |
|---|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | Your name | text | yes |
| 2 | Your position | text | no |
| 3 | Company or business name | text | **yes** |
| 4 | Phone | tel | no |
| 5 | Email | email | yes |
| 6 | Organization type | radio group (sole proprietorship / partnership / LLC / corporation / nonprofit / other) | no |
| 7 | Describe your business | textarea | yes |
| 8 | What do you see as your strong points? | textarea | yes |
| 9 | Where do you feel stress or weakness? | textarea | yes |
| 10 | Is there a specific tool or product you want? | text | no |

Submit button: "Send my pre-check" — sends via email to the
placeholder address (`hello@example.com`) using the mailto fallback until a
form endpoint is configured. Subject line: "Free pre-check request — <company>".

Below the form: a CTA band explaining what happens next (a straight answer on
viability within one business day; the next step is the $250 consultation if
there's a plan), plus the support-vs-total-handoff note for clients lacking
technical knowledge — linking to the contact page.
