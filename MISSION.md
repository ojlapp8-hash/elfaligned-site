## Mission statement

ELFALIGNED LLC is a small business consulting firm that turns a client's own
data into action. We collect and analyze the numbers, documents, and day-to-day
records a business already has — then look across its structure, function,
marketing, administration, organization, and every other facet that limits it —
to find the stress points and weak spots hiding in plain sight.

We benchmark. We research successful businesses in similar sectors and regions
to see what good looks like where you actually operate, compare, and show you
exactly where the gaps are.

Then we close them with technology. Our primary business is technology and
software solutions — existing tools when one exists, custom builds when none
does — including local AI where cloud dependence would be a liability rather
than a feature. We also design and build websites. Our solutions are delivered
through specialized agentic bots tailored to each client from their own data,
so the work fits your business instead of forcing your business to fit a
template — and your data stays safe and secure with you.

Every engagement ends the same way: a detailed plan and a quote to implement,
priced before anything is built, so you know exactly what you are buying and
what it costs before a dollar moves.

## Scope

- **Platforms:** Windows and Android systems only, at this time.
- **Connectivity:** LAN-interconnected network applications between those
  devices.
- **Services:** Data collection and analysis; structural, functional,
  marketing, administrative, and organizational assessment; sector and
  regional benchmarking; technology and software solutions (existing or
