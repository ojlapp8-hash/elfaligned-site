## Mission statement

ELFALIGNED LLC automates the mundane — the repetitive, everyday tasks nobody
wants to do — and governs the AI so it stays safe. It's not about new operating
systems. It's about the copy-paste workflows, the manual bridges, the approvals
that happen over text: the unseen layer of work that eats a small business's
hours.

We start by studying how a client's operation actually runs — where the hours
go, what gets re-typed, what process lives in one person's head. Then we
automate the parts that genuinely pay, in a defined lane a human can check,
and we wrap every automation in governance: who approved it, where the data
goes, and what it costs.

Our primary business is technology and software solutions — existing tools
when one exists, custom builds when none does — including local AI where cloud
dependence would be a liability rather than a feature. A client's own software
(e.g. QuickBooks, their industry tools) stays; we build the layer around it.
The data a client sends us stays on our own systems and never leaves them.
Deliverables are delivered through specialized agentic bots tailored to each
client from their own data, so the work fits the business instead of forcing
the business to fit a template. Deliverables are built to require little or no
training.

Every engagement ends the same way: a detailed plan and a quote to implement,
priced before anything is built, so the client knows exactly what they are
buying and what it costs before a dollar moves.
