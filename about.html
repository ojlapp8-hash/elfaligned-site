About

A consulting firm that happens to build software.

ELFALIGNED LLC is a small business consulting firm based in Gaylord, Michigan. We collect and analyze the data a business already has, find the stress points hiding in plain sight, benchmark against what good looks like in your sector and region, and close the gaps with technology.

Who's behind it

Twenty-two years of running a business before consulting on one.

I spent 22 years operating an equipment dealership in Northern Michigan before becoming disabled in late 2020. That wasn't a detour from this work — it's the reason the work looks the way it does.

Running a dealership means you learn where money actually disappears: the quote that took two days to assemble, the inventory that never quite matched, the process that lived entirely in one person's memory. I've been the customer in that chair. I know what it's like to be sold a "digital transformation" that costs six figures and fixes nothing.

So the approach here is deliberately narrow. Find the real problem from the numbers you already have, price it before it's built, and have someone other than the builder check it works. If the right answer is to buy something off the shelf, you'll hear that too — and I'll tell you which shelf.

The work is remote and documented in writing, because that's how it has to be and because it's better anyway. You get documents you can check, forward, and hold me to.

Our mission

ELFALIGNED LLC turns a client's own data into action. We collect and analyze the numbers, documents, and day-to-day records a business already has — then look across its structure, function, marketing, administration, organization, and every other facet that limits it — to find the stress points and weak spots hiding in plain sight.

We benchmark. We research successful businesses in similar sectors and regions to see what good looks like where you actually operate, compare, and show you exactly where the gaps are.

Then we close them with technology. Our primary business is technology and software solutions — existing tools when one exists, custom builds when none does — including local AI where cloud dependence would be a liability rather than a feature. Solutions are delivered through specialized agentic bots tailored to each client from their own data, so the work fits your business instead of forcing your business to fit a template. Your data stays safe and secure with you.

Every engagement ends the same way: a detailed plan and a quote to implement, priced before anything is built, so you know exactly what you are buying and what it costs before a dollar moves.

Data-driven and evidence-based

Recommendations come from your numbers, not from a deck full of buzzwords.

Honest about existing solutions

When a good tool already exists, we use it. We're not quick to reinvent what already works.

Tailored to your data, sector, and region

Nothing one-size-fits-all. What works for a metro chain may be wrong for a rural shop.
