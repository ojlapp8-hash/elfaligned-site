Approach

Data in, written plan out. And it gets checked.

Most consulting fails before a recommendation is written — because nobody measured the real numbers, and nobody checked the result. Our process fixes both, and it never requires a site visit.

The four steps, in detail

Same sequence every time. Whether the answer turns out to be a plan, a tool, or a website, this is how we get there.

  1. 1 · Collect and analyze your data

    No call needed to start. You send what you already have: the spreadsheet, the system export, the process notes, the records. The better the data we have, the more our team can solve — and the less guesswork ends up in the quote.

    We read it looking across every facet — structure, function, marketing, administration, organization — for the patterns: where hours disappear, which step changes hands twice, where a tool gets abandoned for something simpler, and which private list exists because the official one is unusable. Data shows us the seams more reliably than watching would.

    You get: a written read on where the stress points actually are, ranked by what they cost you, usually within two business days. If what you sent isn't enough to work from, we'll name the two or three specific things that would make it enough.

  2. 2 · Benchmark against your sector and region

    A number on its own tells you nothing. We research successful businesses in similar sectors and regions to see what good looks like where you actually operate, then put your numbers next to it. That's how you find out whether a gap is a real problem or a strength you didn't know you had.

    You get: a comparison showing exactly where you stand — each gap sized in dollars or hours, and each strength named. This step is often the most valuable one, because it stops you spending money fixing things that aren't broken.

  3. 3 · Recommend the fix — existing tool or custom build

    Sometimes the answer is software that already exists, configured properly. Sometimes nothing fits and we build it. Either way the recommendation comes to you in writing, with the cost, before anything is approved. Nobody quietly decides to build something else.

    You get: a recommendation you can act on. If the honest answer is "buy this off-the-shelf product instead," we write that down and tell you which one. We're not in the business of reinventing what already works.

  4. 4 · Deliver the plan and quote, then build it

    Every engagement ends the same way: a detailed plan and a quote to implement, so you know exactly what you're buying and what it costs before a dollar moves. If you want us to build it, the work is delivered through specialized agentic bots configured from your own data.

    The person who built it doesn't get to decide it works. A separate check compares the finished work against what we agreed in step three. If it doesn't pass, it goes back, and you hear about it from us rather than discovering it later.

    You get: something you own outright — the plan, the tools, the documentation — that runs without us, for as long as you want.

Principles

Five rules we don't break.

Data-driven and evidence-based

Recommendations come from your numbers, not from a deck full of buzzwords. If we can't show the evidence, we don't make the claim.

Honest about existing solutions

When a good tool already exists, we use it. We're not quick to reinvent what already works just because building is what we do.

You own the output

The plan, the tools, the documentation — all transfer to you. Nothing reverts, nothing has to be re-rented.

Plain language, in writing

If we can't explain what we're recommending in words a shop owner uses, we don't understand it well enough to recommend it.

Honest "no"

If technology is the wrong answer, we say so on day one and charge you for the analysis only.

"We collect and analyze what a business already has, find the stress points hiding in plain sight, and close them with technology that fits."
