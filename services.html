Services

What we actually do.

Six things, and they run in order: we find what's wrong, we show you how you compare, we recommend the fix, and we build it if you want it built. If your situation doesn't fit one of these, it probably still fits us — tell us what it is.

Data collection & analysis

We collect and analyze the numbers, documents, and day-to-day records your business already has — then look across structure, function, marketing, administration, organization, and every other facet that limits it, to find the stress points and weak spots hiding in plain sight.

  • Spreadsheets, exports, records, process notes
  • Assessed across all six facets, not just operations
  • Every finding ranked by what it costs you
  • Written findings you keep either way

Best fit: an owner who knows something's wrong but not which part.

Sector & regional benchmarking

A number on its own tells you nothing. We research successful businesses in similar sectors and regions to see what good looks like where you actually operate, then show you exactly where the gaps are.

  • Comparison against similar-sector businesses
  • Regional market reality, not national averages
  • Each gap sized in dollars or hours
  • Strengths you didn't know you had

Best fit: deciding whether a problem is actually a problem.

Technology & software solutions

Our primary business. Existing tools when one exists, custom builds when none does. Windows and Android, including LAN-interconnected network applications between your devices.

  • Off-the-shelf where it genuinely fits
  • Custom builds where nothing does
  • Local AI where cloud dependence is a liability
  • Windows and Android platforms

We're not in the business of reinventing what already works.

How solutions are delivered

Specialized agentic bots

Solutions are delivered through specialized agentic bots tailored to each client from their own data — so the work fits your business instead of forcing your business to fit a template. Client data stays safe and secure with you.

  • Configured from your records, not a generic profile
  • Your data stays in your control
  • Built around how you actually operate

Website design

Fast, accessible, search-visible websites — quoted based on the outcome you want. Full detail on the web design page.

  • Quoted on your desired outcome
  • Accessible and fast by default
  • You own the code and the hosting

See the web design page

Planning & quoting

Every engagement ends the same way: a detailed plan and a quote to implement. You know exactly what you're buying and what it costs before a dollar moves.

  • Written plan, itemized
  • Priced before anything is built
  • Yours to keep whether or not you hire us

How it works

How we engage.

Start with the free pre-check. If there's a plan worth pursuing, the next step is the $250 consultation — and you'll know which it is before you spend anything.

Free pre-check

Fill out a short form about your business — what you do, where you think you're strong, and where you feel the strain. It costs nothing, and it tells us both whether a plan is even viable here before anyone commits to paid work.

Free pre-check

Consultation — $250

The full engagement. We collect and analyze your data, benchmark you against successful businesses in your sector and region, and deliver a detailed plan with a quote to implement. Generally handled by phone, email, or data sharing.

You only get here if the pre-check says there's something worth doing.

Site visits, where they're needed

Where physical inspection of hardware or the network is genuinely required, a site visit can be arranged. Those assessments are subcontracted and billed to you at the subcontractor's price — no markup, no padding.

Support, or a total handoff

If your team lacks technical knowledge, we can stay on with support and maintenance — or hand the whole thing over cleanly, documented so you can run it without us. Your choice, decided up front.
