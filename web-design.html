Web design

A website that loads fast, works on every phone, and you actually own.

Built entirely remotely and quoted on the outcome you want. No page-builder subscriptions, no proprietary platform you have to keep renting. You get the code, the hosting, and the documentation.

What you get

Fast by default

Hand-written code, no page-builder bloat. Typical builds land under 250KB total and load in about a second on a phone. Every site ships with a measured performance budget and the numbers to prove it.

  • No WordPress plugin stack
  • No render-blocking tracking scripts
  • Lighthouse report included

Works for everyone

Screen-reader compatible, keyboard navigable, and readable at 200% zoom. High contrast, large touch targets, and no motion that can't be switched off. Accessibility is checked before launch, not requested afterwards.

  • WCAG 2.1 AA as the floor
  • Verified at 320px through 1920px
  • axe-core audit included

Findable on Google

Semantic markup, one clear heading per page, per-page metadata, structured data, and a sitemap. Designed so a small local business can actually be found — not just look good in a screenshot.

  • Per-page titles and descriptions
  • Structured data for local search
  • Search Console submission walkthrough

Yours, not rented

No Wix, no Squarespace, no monthly platform fee. Plain files hosted on free infrastructure that scales. If you ever want another developer, there's nothing to migrate out of.

  • Source code handed over
  • Free-tier hosting, no seat pricing
  • Written documentation

Remote, data-driven

You send the data and review a real preview link in your own browser — nothing depends on a meeting. Approvals happen by email.

  • Preview link at every stage
  • Approvals by email, not call
  • No travel billed

Open source tools only

No licensed fonts. No stock assets.

Everything is free and open source — fonts, icons, tooling. Nothing in your site carries a licence you'd have to renew.

How a build runs

Four steps, same as everything else we do.

  1. Understand the business

    You send what you already have — current site if there is one, product info, photos, and the answers to a short written questionnaire. What do you sell, who buys it, and what should a visitor do when they arrive? You get a written sitemap and content plan before anything is designed.

  2. Design the system

    A design system derived from your brand — colour, type, spacing — rather than a template with your logo pasted in. You review it as a live page in your own browser.

  3. Build and verify

    Every page is checked at six screen widths, audited for accessibility, and measured against the performance budget. The reports come to you in writing.

  4. Launch and hand over

    We deploy it, submit the sitemap, and walk you through editing your own content. You keep the code, the hosting account, and the documentation.
