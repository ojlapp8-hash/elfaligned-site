Work

Four engagements, and what changed after each.

Client names are withheld — most of what we do is operationally sensitive. The shapes are real, and each one ended in a written plan and a quote before anything was built.

Data analysis & custom build · Manufacturing

Shop-floor quote generator

A fabricator quoted every job by hand — measure, price the cut list, write the PDF, email it. Two days per quote on complex work, and quotes went stale waiting. The pricing logic lived entirely in one estimator's head.

Before

Two days per quote. One person, one brain, no backup. Quotes expired before they were sent.

Built

A local Windows tool that turns measurements into a priced cut list and a branded PDF. Runs offline on the shop PC.

After

Ten minutes per quote. The pricing rules are readable code, so a second estimator could learn them in an afternoon.

Scope: 6 weeks · Fixed price · Code and documentation handed over

Data analysis & build · Service company

Private document search assistant

Fourteen years of service histories, warranty terms, and technician notes — searchable only by whoever remembered which folder it was in. A cloud AI assistant was proposed and rejected on privacy grounds by their largest customer.

Before

Average 20 minutes to answer "have we serviced this unit before, and what did we do?"

Built

An on-device assistant over their indexed records. Every answer cites the source document. Nothing leaves the building.

After

Under a minute, with the technician's original note attached. The privacy objection disappeared.

Scope: 5 weeks · Open-weight model on their own server · No per-query cost

Technology solution · Retail

Accounting-to-shop-floor bridge

Point-of-sale, accounting, and the warehouse lived in three systems that met only in a monthly CSV export someone repaired by hand — with a private checklist of the known breakages.

Before

A full day of manual repair every month, plus a permanent inventory discrepancy.

Built

A scheduled local sync with a logged audit trail and alerts on mismatch. Replaces a paid connector they'd outgrown.

After

Zero monthly hours. Inventory reconciles nightly. The private checklist is obsolete.

Scope: 3 weeks · Paid for itself in four months of cancelled subscription

Windows & Android · LAN-linked

Shop-floor tool across the network

A manufacturer running Windows in the office and Android on the floor, with nothing in between. Delivered as agentic bots configured from the shop's own records, connected over the local network.

Problem

Two device types, one operation, and no connection between them. Floor data got retyped into the office system.

Built

A LAN-interconnected toolset — Windows and Android speaking to each other directly, no middleware, no vendor cloud.

Result

No retyping, and the floor keeps working when the internet doesn't. Runs on hardware they already had.

Platforms: Windows and Android · LAN-interconnected · see the full scope
